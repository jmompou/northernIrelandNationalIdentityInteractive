Identities to map

Tick one or more identities. The map shows their combined share of residents.

Source: NISRA, Census 2021, tables MS-B15 (classification 1: each person counted once, in a single category) and MS-B17 (classification 2: every identity a person ticked is counted, so shares can add up to more than 100%). Base: all usual residents.
Boundaries: 2014 wards and Local Government Districts (ONS, generalised to 200 m). Settlements are drawn as circles sized by population, because settlement outlines were not available. Circles with a dashed edge were placed by hand and may be a few hundred metres off. The 460,593 residents outside any settlement ("Elsewhere in NI") have no location and are not drawn.
